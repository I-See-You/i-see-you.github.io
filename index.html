Your history, your choice

Start on your device.
Sync only if you choose.

History stays in Chrome on your device unless you export it or connect a sync destination. Local file sync and Google Drive sync are independent choices; you can use either or both.

What Google Drive access is for

Connecting Drive stores your profile’s website-time history and settings in Tab Timer’s private app-data area. It does not grant access to your other Drive files. Account identification shows which destination you chose and prevents accidental account changes.

The extension has no telemetry or advertising analytics. Read the full policy for what is saved, how optional sync works, and how to delete your data.
