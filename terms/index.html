Using Tab Timer

Tab Timer provides website-time tracking, countdowns, daily limits and optional history sync in Chrome. You control its settings and permissions. Review your timer actions before enabling them, particularly actions that close tabs or interrupt media.

Your information

The privacy policy explains the extension’s storage, optional Google Drive access, retention and deletion. You choose whether to save history, export data or connect a sync destination.

Optional third-party services

Chrome, Google Drive and any file-sync service you select operate under their own terms. Connecting a service does not guarantee continuous availability. Keep a backup of history or settings that you need to retain.

Availability and updates

Features may change as Chrome and connected services change. Tab Timer is provided as available, without a guarantee of uninterrupted operation or exact timing. It is not intended for safety-critical timing.
